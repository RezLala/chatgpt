# Codex production notes

Reusable production notes for the Short workflow. Private scripts, unreleased footage, and detailed review evidence belong in the private production archive.

## Audio and delivery

- Assemble the voice first. Set the narrator and dialogue voices from the production voice card.
- Keep the original pitch. Measure 3.5 words per second per normal row and 3.7 during the climax. Cap routine pauses at 0.10 seconds and leave marked beats at 0.30 seconds. Keep any pitch-preserving tempo adjustment within 1.07×.
- Create caption markers from measured word boundaries, mix voice with effects and music, then target −11 LUFS and a true peak at or below −0.8 dBTP.
- Before delivering, check the combined MP4 for picture, intelligible speech, music, effects, and caption sync.

## Review history

Record each attempted change with its settings, an image or clip that shows the result, what failed, the revised settings, and the status of the next render. Keep character design, color, lighting, and saturation comparisons reproducible from their source code.
