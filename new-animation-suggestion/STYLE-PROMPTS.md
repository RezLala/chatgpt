# Percy style prompts

## Shared scene and performance

Render one 9:16 JPEG in the existing Dollhouse kitchen using the pine worktable, two cream eggs, board, bowl, arch and muted turquoise tile. Use a soft warm key and cool fill. Frame Percy at waist height from a three-quarter angle with a slim continuous silhouette, simple fitted doublet, understated collar, smooth one-piece side-swept hair, small eyes and a crooked smile. His right hand supports the egg while the left hand braces on the table. Carry the gesture from the hip through the torso, shoulder and wrist, with his eyes aimed at the egg.

## Smooth Wood

Sculpt pale beige beech skin with warm chestnut clothing, walnut hair and subdued wood grain. Use softened edges and a matte finish. Build flexible shoulder and elbow deformation into the body. Keep the material quietly wooden in close-up and preserve the expressive eye and mouth controls.

## Soft Clay

Sculpt burnt terracotta clothing, warm peach clay skin and dark umber hair. Use a matte finish with slight surface variation. Keep the torso fitted and the wrist slender. Let the torso gently absorb the recoil. Use restrained eyelid and facial changes against cool turquoise shadows.

## 15-second acting test

| Scene time | Performance |
| --- | --- |
| 0-3.3 s | Walk into the kitchen using alternating world-space foot plants and controlled counter-swing. Settle behind the table. |
| 3.3-6.24 s | Notice the egg, brace the free hand, grip and lift. Let the gaze lead the turn. Shift body weight before bringing the egg to the forehead. |
| 6.24-8.25 s | Break the shell and recoil. Open the lids, lift the brows, and track the yolk. Hold through the narration. |
| 8.25-11.54 s | Percy speaks. Use the timed mouth cues for "Ugh, raw, it's running down my nose and into my best collar." Surprise gives way to irritation. |
| 11.54-15 s | Return to silent posing. Hold the half-lidded glance and settle the shoulders. Keep shell pieces with the hand and the spill on his coat. |
