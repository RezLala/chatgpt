# Prompt Warrior animation generation protocol

Use this protocol for new ChatGPT or Codex animation generations in this project. Read the style prompt and current Dollhouse kit entries before writing shot or cast data.

1. **Block the scene in 3D.** Name the Dollhouse location, time, rig, grounded cast marks, approved camera IDs or test equivalents, and existing prop IDs. Give each action a visible goal at that location. Record floor and work-surface heights, hand contact, sightline, object handoff and release, and what is permitted to move. Use an existing asset for each role where the kit provides it.
2. **Move the character as one body.** Use a continuous weighted silhouette through shoulders, elbows, hips and knees. Blend the hand and neckline into adjoining shapes. Sculpt hair and hats as single forms. Keep a stance foot pinned until its swing. Shift weight through pelvis and chest, then let shoulder, arm, neck, and gaze follow the action. Preview the side and three-quarter angles during the walk and the reach.
3. **Write facial beats in the shot, including silence.** For each beat include the look target, eyelid aperture, brow shape, head angle, and mouth state. Use raised brows and wider lids for surprise, compressed brows and steady focus for brief anger, and a half-lidded side-eye with one raised brow for annoyance. Hold a readable reaction before moving to the next expression. Keep eyes soft and small.
4. **Mark the final voice first.** Record speaker and word timestamps, split major sounds into visible mouth shapes, and blend across neighboring shapes. Use a closed seal for M/B/P, lower lip to upper teeth for F/V, narrow and rounded forms for OO/W, wider forms for EE, open jaw for AH, and a round OH. Keep upper teeth steady and the jaw motion proportional. Set a silent pose between lines, even if narration or another speaker is audible. 5. **Verify from the exported picture.** Review at normal speed and step through footfall, wrist contact, prop transfer and mouth closures. Check wraparound lighting and shadows from multiple angles. Compare a silent expression frame with the approved reference. Save the default stills as JPEG; deliver motion as MP4 and keep the editable source plus timing data.

Dollhouse entries worth checking for a scene like this are L13 Kitchen, C44 lateral action, C73 at the table, C18 close, K01 Walk, K37 Look at prop, K47 Smug, K40 Shock, and K44 Deadpan. The reference test uses custom Three.js poses and framing informed by those entries. A production shot should check the current mark's `ok_cams` before assigning a camera ID.
